Model choice

We shipped the model that generalized

Given 25 Grok images, the 2.36M-weight LoRA memorized "a teammate's phone photo" and flagged 92% of the held-out teammate's real photos. The 2,049-weight probe improved and flagged none, so it ships, and the user's confirmation makes the final call.
